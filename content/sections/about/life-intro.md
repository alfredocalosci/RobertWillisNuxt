---
title: Life and works
---

Born in 1800, Willis’s first book was published anonymously when he was just 21, even before he had gone to University. His keen intellect and problem-solving ability was showcased in his study of the famous chess-playing ‘automaton’, known as ’The Turk’.

Willis was the first to demonstrate that a full-sized player could be concealed within the base of the contraption - as later proved to be the case.
